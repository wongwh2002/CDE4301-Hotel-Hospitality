Semester 1 proof of concept

Prototype & development

A Rokid smart-glasses recognition-and-display prototype, alongside a separate lounge-services scaffold and exploratory preference-extraction experiments.

The service journey

01

Activate

A temple tap starts the session; 15 seconds without detected speech ends it.

02

Recognise & retrieve

Local tracking selects a face; laptop-based recognition retrieves a prepared demo profile.

03

Display

A name and up to two concise fictional preference/allergy cues appear beside the selected face.

System architecture

Implemented path: Rokid RV101 → Wi-Fi/HTTP → laptop recognition → display. The audio-to-preference extension is planned, not yet connected.
