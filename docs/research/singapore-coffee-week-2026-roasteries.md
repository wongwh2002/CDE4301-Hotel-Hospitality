# Singapore Coffee Week 2026: roasteries to compare

**Research date:** 2026-10-24  
**Event:** Singapore Coffee Week 2026, 5–8 November 2026, Sands Expo & Convention Centre, Level 1, Hall C.  
**Scope:** Coffee brands on the event’s official 2026 exhibitor list whose own published material confirms a roasting operation. This is a scheduled exhibitor list, not proof of attendance on the event dates. The organizer describes exhibitors as brands, businesses, and organizations and does not label which are roasters. ## Other listed coffee brands where roasting is unclear

These are examples from the other coffee-related exhibitors whose first-party pages I reviewed. Those pages did not establish that the listed entity roasts its own coffee. This is not an exhaustive review of every coffee-related exhibitor; the event page does not tag exhibitors by business type. They may still be worth visiting; ask who roasted the beans and where.

- **DOAN** sells a house line it calls “DOAN Roast” as well as curated coffees, but its public pages do not establish where or by whom the coffee is roasted. [DOAN brew recipes](https://www.doancompany.kr/RECIPE) · [Coffee selection](https://www.doancompany.kr/)
- **Fifth Wave Coffee** describes itself as specialty coffee and selected brewing tools, but the public store page reviewed did not say it operates a roastery. [Official store](https://fifthwave.coffee/)
- **KenntaRoast** lists roasted coffee products and subscriptions; the public site reviewed did not identify the roasting location or explain whether it roasts in-house. [Official store](https://www.kenntaroast.com/)
- **Balance Coffee** is also an exhibitor, but the Singapore Coffee Association’s profile describes it as a coffee-trading business and café and does not say it roasts in-house. [Association profile](https://www.singaporecoffee.org/SNCC-2025-OFFICIAL-SPONSORS)
- **percolate** describes itself as a curator of coffees and lists beans from international roasters, so it is better compared as a multiroaster retailer than as a roastery. [About Percolate](https://www.percolate.sg/about) · [Coffee shop](https://www.percolate.sg/shop/)
- **The Coffee Folks SG** sells brewing equipment and offers subscriptions featuring beans roasted by local roasters; its site does not identify it as a roaster. [Subscription details](https://thecoffeefolkssg.com/subscription/) · [Product catalogue](https://thecoffeefolkssg.com/products/)

## Quick comparison prompts for the event

1. Try the same brew method across local roasters, then compare roast date, origin, processing and flavor notes on the bags.
2. Ask whether the Singapore-listed brand roasts locally, imports roasted coffee, or sells beans roasted by a partner—especially for Baristart and the unclear-status brands above.
3. Compare product focus: blends and single origins (Bettr, Cowpresso, Suzuki), Indonesian-origin coffee (Tanamera), direct-fire roasting (Soon), espresso/filter distinctions (Zero), and roastery services or training (Kings Cart, Lighthouse, Parchmen).

Prices are omitted because published listings use different currencies, bag sizes and sales channels; the event page does not publish event prices.
